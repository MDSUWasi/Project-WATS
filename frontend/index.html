What am I looking at?

Each frame is a SPHEREx Level-2 spectral image: a 2-D image of the sky in which every pixel has its own infrared wavelength (0.75–5 µm). Project WATS reprojects the six detector arrays onto a common north-up grid and combines them into a colour composite, so a single click gives you a multi-band view of the same patch of sky.

How to spot moving objects

  1. Press play and watch the field. Stars stay fixed; asteroids, comets, brown dwarfs and planets drift.
  2. Turn on Subtract to see the difference between consecutive frames — movers appear as a positive/negative pair.
  3. Turn on Blink A/B to flip between the first and last visit, then increase the stretch.
  4. Use Colour composite to separate cool (red) from warm (blue) sources.

Data & credits

SPHEREx is a NASA Astrophysics Medium Explorer mission. Data are retrieved live from the SPHEREx Archive at IRSA (DR1/QR3). Auxiliary catalogues: Gaia DR3 and CatWISE2020. Images are rendered client-side; no data are uploaded.
